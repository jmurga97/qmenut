# Unanswered stamp requests are accepted automatically

Guests claim stamps with a stamp request that staff accept, accept fewer of, or decline. A
request nobody answers within 12 hours is accepted automatically, at the quantity the guest
claimed. The rotating venue code already proves the guest was at the branch, and the stamp
request limit caps the claim, so staff silence costs the restaurant at most that many stamps
for one visit, while the guest always gets an answer. The restaurant keeps control by
answering within the 12 hours or by undoing an accepted request afterwards, unless the guest
has already spent those stamps.

## Considered Options

- **Expire unanswered requests**: rejected; it brings back the problem that made the July
  2026 design drop its approval queue. When nobody watches the tablet, the guest's first
  experience of the program is stamps that disappear.
- **Leave them pending until someone answers**: rejected; the guest's card shows stamps they
  can never spend, and the staff list fills with requests nobody remembers.
- **Accept at once, as before, with staff declining afterwards**: rejected; the restaurant
  asked to approve the quantity, and a guest could spend stamps before staff saw the claim.
